Complete Heating & Cooling Services for Roanoke Properties

We service every type of heating and cooling equipment you'll find in local homes and businesses, from central air conditioners and gas furnaces to heat pumps and ductless mini-splits. It doesn't matter whether the building is a starter home, a townhome, or a storefront. Our technicians understand the local weather, the way homes here were built, and the code requirements inspectors enforce, and that knowledge shows up in every diagnosis.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or a seasonal tune-up. Repairs are usually straightforward: a failed capacitor, a corroded flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a seized blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling itself to an early death. Tune-ups cover coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, which does more than anything else to extend equipment life.

For commercial property owners and managers nearby, we service the rooftop units, split systems, and dedicated ventilation equipment that restaurants, retail spaces, offices, and multi-unit buildings depend on. Our commercial team understands that a hot dining room or a freezing office means lost business, so we prioritize fast diagnosis, stock common parts on the truck, and schedule work around your operating hours whenever we can.

Two situations call for a quick step from you before our technician gets there. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or gives off a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and give us a call. That small step can prevent a much larger repair.

Keeping Your Roanoke HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

How old is your system? That number says a lot about what comes next. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
