Two common situations call for a quick step from you before a technician arrives. The second is a furnace that short-cycles or produces a burning smell. Shut it down at the switch and call us. That small step can prevent a far bigger repair.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Newport News

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that runs for years and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, two feet of clearance around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, central air typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, ignored for a season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Comfort Challenges Newport News Homeowners Face

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, a musty smell at startup. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We find the root cause instead of treating symptoms, so small problems stay small.
